How it works

Each round, you and 5–11 bot players stake a fixed tier (100K / 300K / 500K / 1M / 2M / 5M / 10M AGC) and drop one ball through 8 rows of pegs into one of 9 slots. Each slot has a multiplier (edges 11.6×, center 0.4×); the table is tuned so the expected multiplier is exactly 1.0.

Your score = stake × multiplier. 0.3% of the pot is burned and 0.3% is an operational fee (0.6% total house edge); the other 99.4% is split in proportion to score:

payout = floor(0.994 × pot × score / totalScore)

All money math is integer-only (multipliers ×100, fees in basis points, BigInt) — the same formula proven in verify.py over 20,000 simulated rounds: payouts + burn + fee + dust always equal the pot exactly. If a round ever had fewer than 2 players, everyone is refunded in full with no burn and no fee.

In production, randomness would come from one Chainlink VRF word per round (provably fair — the proof is verified by the contract itself), with each player's slot derived as the popcount of the first 8 bits of keccak256(word, roundId, player) — see DESIGN.md.
