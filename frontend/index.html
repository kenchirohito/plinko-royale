How to play

Rounds run every 1 minute. Pick a fixed stake (100K / 300K / 500K / 1M / 2M / 5M / 10M AGC), one entry per address per round. After the round closes, anyone can press Draw — Chainlink VRF produces one random number that decides every player's slot (the ball drops through 8 rows of pegs into 9 slots; edge multiplier 11.6×, center 0.4×).

Settle splits the pot: 0.3% burned, 0.3% operational fee, 99.4% paid to players in proportion to score (stake × multiplier). Payouts go to your game balance — withdraw anytime. Rounds with <2 players, or that are not drawn / get no random number within 1 hour, can be fully refunded.
